Lock missile: keep an enemy inside the dashed circle in front of your nose and within your jet's lock range (on its card; stealth jets must be closer) — the box turns red and you hear a steady tone. Each jet has its own reloads. When the MISSILE warning flashes, press F: flares pull most incoming lock-on missiles away. Cobra: while fast, hold S and pull (↓) or turn (A/D) — the nose rears up or swings sideways and the jet brakes hard. Let go to drop the nose and recover. Don't fly into the city's skyscrapers.
